Data Safety & Transmission / سلامة ونقل البيانات

Unlike static local-only apps, PlantCare AI utilizes network-based features to identify plants, analyze health, and support development. Here is the accurate breakdown of our data safety actions:

1. Photos & Captured Images / الصور الملتقطة

Data Type: Photos and video frames.

Handling: When you capture/upload a plant photo to scan or analyze its health, this image is transmitted securely over HTTPS directly to your selected third-party AI service (e.g., Google Gemini, OpenRouter, or OpenAI).

Retention: The application publisher does not collect, receive, or store your photos on any proprietary servers. The chosen AI services handle images according to their respective privacy terms.

2. Text Prompts & User Queries / المدخلات النصية

Data Type: User text messages and plant health questions.

Handling: Text messages entered in the AI Doctor screen are sent over secure network connections to process appropriate diagnoses and recommend treatment actions.

Retention: Plant diaries and clinical histories are saved strictly off-line in your device's private local state. Only the active context prompts are sent to selected AI services.

3. Device Identifiers & Performance Logs / معرفات الأجهزة والتحليلات

Data Type: Device IDs, approximate IP, ad interaction metrics, and crash diagnostics.

Handling: If mobile ads are present, Google AdMob and Firebase services may gather anonymous device identifiers (such as Android Advertising ID - AAID) and performance events to serve suitable, non-intrusive advertisements and monitor application crashes.

Security & Local Storage / التشغيل والتخزين المحلي

Encryption & Keys:

If you provide a personal API key for custom AI setups, the key is encrypted using AES-256 and stored using secure Capacitor native persistent containers. It is never exposed or collected.

Offline Records:

All details about your personal local plant library, custom watering timer preferences, and reminder logs are stored offline inside standard private storage.
