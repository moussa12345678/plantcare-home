01

Introduction

Welcome to PlantCare AI. Your privacy is strongly protected. This Privacy Policy describes how we handle the information collected by this application.

We believe in complete transparency about how your data is used, stored, and protected. This document outlines our practices in detail.

02

Data Collection & Storage

Local Storage

All your data, including your plant library, health records, and settings, are stored locally on your device. We do not maintain any central servers or databases to store your personal information.

API Keys

If you provide an API key for AI services, it is encrypted using AES and stored securely on your local device using native secure storage mechanisms. It is never transmitted to our servers (because we don't have any). It is only used to directly communicate with the AI provider you selected (e.g., Google Gemini, OpenRouter, or OpenAI).

Camera & Images

The app requires camera access to scan plants. Images captured are sent directly to the selected AI provider to identify the plant or analyze its health. We do not store, view, or retain copies of your images.

03

Third-Party Services & Affiliate Links

We use third-party services which have their own privacy policies:

  • AI Providers: Services like Google Gemini or OpenAI process the images and text prompts to return plant data. Please refer to their respective privacy policies to understand how they handle your data.
  • AdMob: If advertisements are configured in your version of the application, we may use Google AdMob to display advertisements. AdMob may use device identifiers and other information for serving appropriate ads. You can review Google's advertising privacy policies for details.

Amazon Affiliate Disclosure

Affiliate Partnership

PlantCare AI is a participant in the Amazon Services LLC Associates Program, an affiliate advertising program designed to provide a means for sites and apps to earn advertising fees by advertising and linking to Amazon.com and affiliated sites.

When you click on recommended care products (such as fertilizers, pesticides, or accessories) within this application, you may be redirected to Amazon where we earn a small commission from qualifying purchases at absolutely no extra cost to you.

04

Changes to this Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Since the app functions primarily offline/locally, material changes will apply in subsequent app updates.

We encourage you to review this policy periodically to stay informed about how we protect your information.
